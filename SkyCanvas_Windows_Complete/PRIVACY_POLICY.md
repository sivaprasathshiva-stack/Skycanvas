# Privacy Policy for SkyCanvas

**Effective Date**: June 25, 2026  
**Last Updated**: June 25, 2026

## Introduction

SkyCanvas ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use SkyCanvas.

## Information We DO NOT Collect

SkyCanvas is designed with privacy-first principles:

- ❌ **No personal information** - We don't collect names, emails, or contact details
- ❌ **No location tracking** - Location coordinates you enter are processed locally and never transmitted
- ❌ **No usage analytics** - We don't track how you use the app
- ❌ **No crash reports** - No automatic error reporting to external servers
- ❌ **No advertising** - No ad networks, no tracking pixels
- ❌ **No cloud storage** - All data stays on your device
- ❌ **No user accounts** - No registration, no login required

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device:

- **Location settings** - Saved on your device only
- **Time preferences** - Stored locally
- **App settings** - Local storage only

You can delete all data by uninstalling the app.

## External Data Sources

SkyCanvas downloads astronomy data from:

- **NASA JPL** - Planetary position data (public domain)
- **Yale Bright Star Catalog** - Star positions (public domain)

These downloads are one-time and do not transmit any personal information.

## Network Access

SkyCanvas requires internet access only for:

- **Initial setup** - Downloading astronomy data files (~17 MB)
- **Casting** - Local network communication with Chromecast/AirPlay devices

No data is sent to external servers during normal operation.

## Children's Privacy

SkyCanvas is safe for children. We do not collect any information from users of any age.
